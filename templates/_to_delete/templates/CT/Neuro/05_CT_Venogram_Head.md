## Findings
No intracranial haemorrhage. No midline shift, transtentorial or tonsillar herniation. Ventricles and basal cisterns are patent. Grey-white matter differentiation is preserved. Midline (incl sella and pituitary gland) and posterior fossa structures are unremarkable.

Superior sagittal, inferior sagittal, transverse, sigmoid, and straight sinuses are patent. Internal cerebral veins are patent. 
Major cortical and proximal internal jugular veins are patent. Intracranial arteries opacify normally for phase of study.

Orbits are clear. Mastoid air cells are appropriately aerated.

## Impression
No intracranial haemorrhage.
No dural venous sinus thrombosis.
