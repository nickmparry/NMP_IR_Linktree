## Findings:
**Head:** 
No intracranial haemorrhage. No midline shift, transtentorial or tonsillar herniation. Ventricles and basal cisterns are patent. Grey-white matter differentiation is preserved. Midline (incl sella and pituitary gland) and posterior fossa structures are unremarkable.

Orbits are clear. Mastoid air cells are appropriately aerated. No calvarial or base of skull fracture.

**C-spine:** 
No prevertebral soft tissue thickening. Appropriate alignment of the cervical spine. Craniocervical junction is intact. No abnormal widening of IV disc spaces, facet joints, or spinous processes. No cervical vertebral or occipital condyle fracture. Aerodigestive tract is patent. 

**Chest:** 
No pneumothorax or pleural effusion. No pulmonary laceration or contusion. No pericardial effusion. No traumatic aortic injury. No pneumomediastinum. No CT definable oesophageal injury. No subcutaneous emphysema. 

No sternal, clavicular, or scapular fracture. No thoracic vertebral fracture. No rib fracture.

**Abdomen/pelvis:** 
No pneumoperitoneum or intra-abdominal free fluid. Liver, spleen, kidneys, adrenals, pancreas, gallbladder are unremarkable. Bladder and remainder of pelvic structures are within normal limits. No discernible bowel or mesenteric injury noting these can be occult on imaging.

No lumbosacral fracture. Pelvis is intact. No pubic symphysis or SIJ widening. No proximal femoral fracture.

## Impression:
No intracranial haemorrhage. No skull or cervical vertebral fracture. 
No thoracic injury.
No abdominal injury.
