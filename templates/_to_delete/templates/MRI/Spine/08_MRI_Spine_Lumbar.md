### Normal
No significant disc bulge, spinal canal stenosis or foraminal narrowing.

### Sequence guide
- Alignment / vertebral body height — sagittal T1/T2
- Vertebral marrow signal (oedema, fracture, lesion) — sagittal T1, STIR
- Conus medullaris level / signal — sagittal T1/T2
- Disc hydration / height (degeneration) — sagittal T2
- Disc bulge / protrusion / extrusion — axial T2, sagittal T2
- Central canal stenosis — axial T2, sagittal T2
- Lateral recess / neural foraminal stenosis — axial T2, parasagittal T1/T2
- Facet arthropathy — axial T2
- Cauda equina / nerve root crowding — axial T2, sagittal T2
- Paraspinal soft tissues — axial/sagittal T2, STIR
