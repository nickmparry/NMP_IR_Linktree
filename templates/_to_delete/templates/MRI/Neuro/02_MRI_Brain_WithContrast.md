## Clinical:


## Technique:
MRI brain, pre- and post-contrast.

## Comparison:


## Findings:
No abnormal restricted diffusion. No susceptibility artefact to suggest intracranial haemorrhage. No discernible mass, midline shift, transtentorial or tonsiller herniation. Ventricles and basal cisterns are patent. Grey-white matter differentiation is preserved. No enhancing mass lesion or leptomeningeal enhancement. No enhancing mass lesion.

Major intracranial flow voids are preserved. Sella, pituitary gland and posterior fossa structures are unremarkable. No calvarial marrow signal abnormality.
