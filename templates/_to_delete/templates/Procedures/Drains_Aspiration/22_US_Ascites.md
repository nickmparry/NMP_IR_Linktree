# US Ascites

## Clinical:


## Procedure:
Written and verbal consent.
Standard operating sterility adhered to.
Performed under US guidance.
Suitable pocket of ascites identified in the RIGHT/LEFT lower quadrant.
Total 10ml 1% lignocaine to skin and peritoneum overlying the entry site.
18g needle advanced into the peritoneal cavity. Wire exchange. 
8.5fr multipurpose drain inserted via seldinger technique.
Straw coloured fluid draining freely. X ml sample sent for pathology.
Drain secured to skin and connected to drainage bag.
Patient tolerated procedure well.
No periprocedural complications.

## Comment:
US-guided ascites drain as described
Drainage instructions and management as per treating team.
Drain clamped for transport.
