# CT Genicular Block

## Clinical:


## Procedure:
Written and verbal consent.
Standard operating sterility adhered to.
Performed under CT guidance.
Expected location of the superolateral, superomedial, inferomedial genicular nerves identified.
Total 3ml 1% lignocaine to skin and soft tissue overlying each nerve.
25g needle advanced adjacent to the nerve and 2ml 0.5% marcain injected.
Patient tolerated procedure well, reports reduction in general pain. 
No periprocedural complications.

## Comment:
Diagnostic block of RIGHT/LEFT genicular nerves performed as described.
