# Ultrasound Liver Screening

## Clinical History:

## Technique:
Greyscale ultrasound of the liver with assessment of echogenicity relative to the right renal cortex and diaphragm, and posterior beam attenuation.

## Comparison:

## Findings:
**Liver:** Diffusely increased echotexture (mild/moderate/severe). Smooth capsular margin. No focal hepatic lesion. No intrahepatic biliary dilatation. Portal and hepatic veins are patent with normal direction of flow. No ascites.
**Gallbladder:** Normal wall thickness. No cholelithiasis or pericholecystic fluid. CBD of normal calibre.
**Pancreas:** Visualised portions unremarkable / obscured by bowel gas.
**Spleen:** Normal size (< 13 cm), homogeneous echotexture.
**Right kidney:** Good corticomedullary differentiation. No hydronephrosis. No suspicious mass lesion.
**Left kidney:** Good corticomedullary differentiation. No hydronephrosis. No suspicious mass lesion.
**Aorta:** Normal calibre. 

## Impression:
Diffuse hepatic steatosis, [mild/moderate/severe] grade, by ultrasound.
No focal hepatic lesion.
