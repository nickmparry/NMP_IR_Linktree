## Procedure:
Written and verbal consent.
Standard operating sterility adhered to.
Total [_]ml 1% lignocaine to skin and soft tissue overlying the port-a-cath pocket.
Incision made over the previous scar and the port chamber dissected free from surrounding fibrous tissue. 
[_] x sutures removed in full. Catheter and hub removed.
Pocket closed with 3-0 vicryl deep interrupted and running superficial sutures.
Dressings applied.
Patient tolerated procedure well. No periprocedural complications.

## Anaesthetic:
Midazolam [_]mg IV.
Fentanyl [_]mcg IV.

## Post-Procedure:
Post-sedation observations as per protocol. 
Can discharge if well, no review required.
