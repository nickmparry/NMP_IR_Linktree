# CT Shoulder Ablation

## Clinical:


## Procedure:
Written and verbal consent.
Standard operating sterility adhered to.
Performed under CT guidance.
LEFT/RIGHT suprascapular, axillary, and lateral pectoral nerves identified.
Total 3ml 1% lignocaine to skin and soft tissue overlying each nerve.
RF electrode advanced adjacent to each nerve. Sensory and motor testing performed.
Mixture of dexamethasone (8mg/2ml) and 4ml 0.5% bupivacaine distributed equally to each nerve prior to ablation.
Ablation performed at 80 degrees for 90 seconds.
Patient tolerated procedure well.
No periprocedural complications.

## Comment:
RF ablation of the RIGHT/LEFT shoulder joint supply as described.
