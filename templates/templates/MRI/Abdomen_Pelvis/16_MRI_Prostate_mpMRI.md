# MRI Prostate (mpMRI)

## Clinical:


## Technique:
Multiparametric MRI prostate (T2, DWI/ADC, DCE sequences), per PI-RADS protocol.

## Comparison:


## Findings:
Prostate gland is normal in size for age, with preserved zonal anatomy. No discrete lesion on T2, DWI/ADC or dynamic contrast-enhanced sequences (PI-RADS 1). No extraprostatic extension, seminal vesicle invasion or capsular breach. Seminal vesicles are unremarkable. No pelvic lymphadenopathy or suspicious osseous lesion within the field of view.

### Checklist
- Prostate volume (mL) / PSA density
- Zonal anatomy assessed (peripheral, transition, central)
- Lesion(s): location (sector map), size, T2 score, DWI/ADC score, DCE (+/-)
- Overall PI-RADS category per lesion (1–5)
- Extraprostatic extension
- Seminal vesicle invasion
- Neurovascular bundle involvement
- Pelvic lymph nodes
- Bones within field of view
