### Sequence guide
- Primary tract course — high-resolution axial T2, axial/coronal STIR or T2 fat-sat
- Internal / external opening — axial and coronal T2, STIR
- Sphincter integrity (IAS/EAS) — high-resolution axial T2, axial T1
- Abscess / collection — STIR, T2 fat-sat, post-contrast T1 fat-sat if given
- Inflammatory activity / enhancement — STIR, post-contrast T1 fat-sat
- Horseshoe / secondary tracts — axial and coronal STIR or T2 fat-sat
